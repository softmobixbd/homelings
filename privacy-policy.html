Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026 · Applies to the Homelings app on Android (Google Play) and this website.

In short: Homelings has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts. Your game progress stays on your device. On phones, the game uses Google services for crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics), game statistics (Firebase Analytics), remote settings (Firebase Remote Config), ads (Google AdMob) and the optional "Remove ads" purchase (Google Play). In the EU, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.

2. Data that stays on your device

The game saves the following only on your device. We do not receive it.

  • Your progress: stars for each level, which worlds and levels are open, and which rule cards you have seen.
  • Your settings: sound, music, vibration and language.
  • Your hint balance, and whether "Remove ads" is active.
  • When the game last asked for a store review or offered an update, so it doesn't ask too often.
  • Your consent choices for ads and statistics, saved by Google's consent tool (the IAB TCF consent string, where it applies).

You can delete this data at any time with Settings → Reset progress, by clearing the app's storage in your phone's settings, or by uninstalling the game. See Delete your data.

4. Why we use data, and legal bases

  • To run the game and keep your progress: on your device only.
  • To find and fix crashes (Crashlytics): our legitimate interest in a game that works.
  • To understand and improve the game (Analytics): your consent where the law requires it (EU, UK, Switzerland), otherwise our legitimate interest in improving the game.
  • To deliver game settings (Remote Config): our legitimate interest in adjusting the game without forcing an update.
  • To show ads that fund the free game (AdMob): your consent for personalised ads and for using your device's identifiers where the law requires it; otherwise limited or non-personalised ads, as Google's policies allow.
  • To provide a purchase you make (Google Play): performing our agreement with you.

5. Your choices and consent

  • EU, UK and Switzerland: before ads load, Google's consent form asks for your choices. Until you answer, statistics are set to "denied" (Google consent mode). Analytics and ad features then follow exactly what you accepted. You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Remove ads: the one-time purchase turns off the banner and between-level ads. Optional hint videos stay available.
  • Stop all collection: uninstall the game. Puzzles also play offline. Statistics from offline play are kept on the device and sent the next time you are online.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we do not share it with anyone except the Google services listed above, which process it to provide their service to us. Showing personalised ads through Google AdMob may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some US state privacy laws. You can opt out with the advertising settings described in section 5, or by declining personalised ads in the consent form where it is shown.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

  • Firebase Analytics: event data is kept for the retention period set in our Google Analytics property: [2 months / 14 months: confirm in Google Analytics → Data settings → Data retention].
  • Firebase Crashlytics and Remote Config, Google AdMob and Google Play: for the periods described in Google's and Firebase's privacy documentation linked in section 3.

8. International transfers

Google processes data in the United States and other countries. Where data from the EU, UK or Switzerland is transferred, Google relies on approved transfer mechanisms such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, as described in its terms.

9. Age 13+ and children

Homelings is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 is using the game, please contact us and we will help with deletion as far as we can (see Delete your data).

In some EU countries the age at which you can consent to personalised ads on your own is higher than 13 (up to 16). If that applies to you, please ask a parent or guardian before accepting personalised ads in the consent form.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to complain to your data protection authority.

Because Homelings has no accounts, we do not know who you are, and the statistics and crash data we see are not linked to your name. In most cases we cannot identify records that relate to you. To exercise your rights or ask a question, email support@homelings.io and we will explain what we can do. You can always delete everything stored on your device yourself, and withdraw consent in Settings → Privacy choices.

11. Security

Data sent to Google services travels over encrypted connections. Your progress on the device is protected by your phone's own security. No method is perfectly secure, but we use well-maintained services and keep the game updated.
