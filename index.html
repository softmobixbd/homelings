Homelings

Every little one needs a way home.

A calm colour-routing puzzle. Tap a critter and it travels along the path to the home of its colour. But the path is shared, and whoever goes first changes everything for the rest. Find the right order.

How it plays

One tap. One path. The order is the puzzle.

There are no timers and no lives. Each level is a small board you can solve in a minute or two, if you spot who has to go first.

  1. Tap a critter

    It travels along the vines to the farthest free home of its colour, all by itself.

  2. Others are in the way

    Critters and full homes block the path. Send the wrong one first and someone gets sealed in.

  3. Find the order

    Get everyone home. Undo, restart or ask for a hint whenever you like.

Questions

Good to know

Is Homelings free?

Yes. The game is free to play with ads: a small banner on the level list and an occasional ad between levels (never in the middle of a level). You can watch an optional short video to get extra hints. A one-time "Remove ads" purchase turns off the banner and the between-level ads.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. Every puzzle plays offline. Ads, crash reports and game statistics only work when you are online.

Who is it for?

Homelings is a logic puzzle for players aged 13 and up. It is not designed for young children.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts. Your progress is saved on your device only. See how data is handled.

Who makes Homelings?

SoftMobiXBD, a small independent game studio in Bangladesh. You can find our other games on Google Play. For questions or feedback, write to softmobixbd@gmail.com.

Which languages are supported?

English, Bangla, Hindi, Urdu, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil) and Indonesian. The game follows your phone's language, and you can change it in Settings.

Ready to guide them home?

Short puzzles, big "aha" moments. Play a level or two whenever you have a minute.
