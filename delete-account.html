Your data

Delete your account and data

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Homelings has no accounts. You never sign up or log in, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number. There is no account to delete. This page explains how to delete the data that does exist.

1. Your game progress (on your device)

Stars, unlocked levels, settings and your hint balance are stored only on your phone. To delete them, either:

  • In the game, open Settings → Reset progress and confirm. This clears your stars and unlocked levels.
  • Or, to remove everything the game stores (progress, settings and saved consent choices), open your phone's Settings → Apps → Homelings → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the game.

We don't have a copy of your progress, so once deleted it can't be recovered.

2. The "Remove ads" purchase

Purchases are recorded by Google Play under your Google account, not by us. Deleting the game's data doesn't cancel a purchase: after reinstalling, use Settings → Restore purchases. For refunds or your Google Play purchase history, see Google Play Help.

3. Game statistics and crash reports (Firebase)

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics record how the game is played and when it crashes, using random app identifiers rather than your name. You can:

  • Stop new collection: uninstall the game. In the EU, UK and Switzerland you can also withdraw consent for statistics in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Let it expire: statistics are deleted automatically after the retention period described in our Privacy Policy.
  • Ask us: email support@homelings.io with the subject "Data deletion request". Because the data isn't linked to your name, we usually can't tell which records are yours, but we will reply within 30 days to explain what we can delete.

4. Advertising data (Google AdMob)

Ads use your device's advertising ID where you allow it. To cut the link between your device and past ad activity, reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device). To manage data Google holds about you, see Google Account → Data & privacy and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. What we keep

We do not keep any other data about you. If you email us, we keep your message only as long as needed to answer it and for our records, then delete it.
